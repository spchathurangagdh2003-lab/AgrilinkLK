About AgriLink LK

  • Connects Sri Lankan farmers directly with buyers, businesses, and consumers through a secure digital platform.
  • Eliminates intermediaries and unnecessary costs, ensuring fair prices for farmers and affordable rates for buyers.
  • Supports and empowers local farmers by giving them better market access and control over pricing.
  • Provides a transparent and trusted marketplace with verified users, clear product information, and direct communication.
  • Uses modern web technologies to improve efficiency, reduce waste, and modernize the agricultural supply chain.
  • Encourages sustainable farming practices and strengthens rural communities across Sri Lanka.
